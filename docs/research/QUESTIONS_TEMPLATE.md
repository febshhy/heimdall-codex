# Research questions template

> Template only. The prompts below are examples, not confirmed gaps in your project.

## Question: [specific uncertainty]

- Status: Open
- Owner: [person or team]
- Why it matters: [decision or risk affected]
- Known facts and sources: [links]
- Unknowns: [facts or tests needed]
- Next action: [experiment, interview, search, or measurement]
- Decision due: [date, if applicable]

## Prompts to consider when relevant

- Which user job must the first release support?
- What data must persist, and who owns it?
- Which callers, interfaces, and integrations depend on this behavior?
- What authentication, authorization, privacy, and retention requirements apply?
- Which failure modes, recovery objectives, and rollback steps matter?
- What checks are necessary to accept or reject the proposed behavior?
